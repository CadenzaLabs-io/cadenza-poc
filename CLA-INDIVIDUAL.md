## How to sign

Signing is done through the Cadenza Labs CLA bot on GitHub. When You open a pull request against a Cadenza repository, the bot will prompt You to review and sign this Agreement by commenting on Your pull request. Your signature is recorded once per GitHub account and applies to all future contributions.

If You prefer to sign out-of-band (for example, if Your organization requires a wet signature), email a signed copy to **legal@cadenzalabs.dev** with:

- Your full name
- Your GitHub username
- Your email address
- Your postal address
- The name of any employer, if You are contributing on behalf of one (in which case Your employer should also execute the [Corporate CLA](./CLA-CORPORATE.md))
