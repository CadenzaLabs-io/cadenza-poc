## Defensive posture and permitted uses

The Cadenza patent portfolio is intended to secure operating room for the Cadenza rail, not to obstruct the broader ecosystem. Cadenza Labs commits to the following defensive posture:

- **Consumers of the SDK's public interfaces do not require a separate patent licence.** Applications, wallets, merchant integrations, issuer connectors, and services that consume Cadenza's public interfaces — rather than reimplementing the underlying methods — are free to build on top of Cadenza without further patent authorisation. The pending patents cover the *methods and systems implemented in the SDK*, not the interfaces exposed to consumers of it.
- **Personal, evaluation, academic, and internal-organisational use is permitted under the MIT licence alone.** No separate patent grant is required for these uses.
- **Standards-body participation.** Where Cadenza Labs contributes patented subject matter to an open standard through a recognised standards body (for example, an IETF working group, W3C, DIF, FIDO Alliance, Linux Foundation, or comparable), the applicable RAND / FRAND commitments of that body will apply to the contributed subject matter as scoped to implementation of that standard. Specific disclosures and commitments will be added to this notice at the time each such contribution is made.
- **No assertion against passive infringement.** Cadenza Labs will not initiate patent litigation solely to obstruct legitimate research, educational use, competing standardisation efforts, or defensive-patent-aggregation participation.
- **Reciprocal termination.** Any party that initiates patent litigation against Cadenza Labs, its licensees, or the Cadenza SDK forfeits any implied patent licence otherwise available under this notice as of the date such litigation is filed.

Where a separate commercial patent licence is required — hosted competitors, embedded commercial products, resellers, and similar commercial deployments — enquiries can be directed to `licensing@cadenzalabs.dev`.
