# Cadenza — Corporate Contributor License Agreement (CCLA)

**Version 1.1 · effective 2026-07-25**

This Corporate Contributor License Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between the Corporation identified in Section 10 ("Corporation") and Cadenza Agentic Solutions Inc., a Delaware corporation doing business as Cadenza Labs (referred to as "Cadenza Labs" or "We" or "Us").

This Agreement covers Contributions submitted by the Corporation and by the individuals designated by the Corporation. It is required in addition to any Individual CLA that the Corporation's designated contributors sign, to ensure clean intellectual-property provenance for Corporate contributions to the Cadenza open-source project.

## 1. Definitions

**"Corporation"** means the entity identified in Section 10 and its subsidiaries, if applicable.

**"Contribution"** means the code, documentation, or other original works of authorship expressly identified by the Corporation, as well as any original work of authorship, including any modifications or additions to an existing work, that is intentionally submitted by the Corporation to Us for inclusion in, or documentation of, the Project. "Submitted" has the meaning given in Section 1 of the Individual CLA.

**"Project"** means the Cadenza open-source project, currently hosted at github.com/CadenzaLabs-io and any related repositories that We designate as part of the Project.

**"Designated Employees"** means the employees, contractors, or other agents of the Corporation who are authorized by the Corporation to submit Contributions on the Corporation's behalf, as listed in Section 10 or as updated by the Corporation from time to time by written notice to Us.

## 2. Grant of Copyright Licence

Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, the Corporation hereby grants to Us and to recipients of software distributed by Us a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable copyright licence to reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display, publicly perform, sublicense, and distribute Contributions submitted by the Corporation and its Designated Employees, and such derivative works.

## 3. Grant of Patent Licence

Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, the Corporation hereby grants to Us and to recipients of software distributed by Us a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable (except as stated in this section) patent licence to make, have made, use, offer to sell, sell, import, and otherwise transfer the Project, where such licence applies only to those patent claims licensable by the Corporation that are necessarily infringed by a Contribution alone or by combination of a Contribution with the Project to which such Contribution was submitted.

If any entity institutes patent litigation against the Corporation or any other entity (including a cross-claim or counterclaim in a lawsuit) alleging that a Contribution, or the Project to which the Corporation has contributed, constitutes direct or contributory patent infringement, then any patent licences granted to that entity under this Agreement for that Contribution or Project shall terminate as of the date such litigation is filed.

## 4. Right to Re-Licence

The Corporation acknowledges and agrees that We may re-licence the Project (including the Corporation's Contributions) under alternative licence terms in future, including but not limited to a commercial licence or a dual-licence arrangement, and that the licences granted in Sections 2 and 3 above expressly permit such re-licensing without further consent from the Corporation or additional compensation to the Corporation.

## 5. Corporation Representations

The Corporation represents that:

(a) each Contribution submitted by the Corporation or its Designated Employees is either the Corporation's original creation, or the Corporation has secured all necessary rights from third parties to enable it to grant the licences set out in Sections 2 and 3;

(b) the Corporation has full corporate power and authority to enter into this Agreement, and the individual signing this Agreement on the Corporation's behalf has full authority to bind the Corporation;

(c) each Designated Employee is authorized to submit Contributions on behalf of the Corporation and to bind the Corporation to the terms of this Agreement in respect of such Contributions; and

(d) Contribution submissions include complete details of any third-party licence or other restriction (including, but not limited to, related patents and trademarks) of which the Corporation is aware and which are associated with any part of the Contributions.

## 6. Warranty Disclaimer

The Corporation is not expected to provide support for Contributions, except to the extent the Corporation desires to provide support. Contributions are provided on an "AS IS" BASIS, WITHOUT WARRANTIES OR CONDITIONS OF ANY KIND, either express or implied, including, without limitation, any warranties or conditions of TITLE, NON-INFRINGEMENT, MERCHANTABILITY, or FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 7. Third-Party Submissions

Should the Corporation wish to submit work that is not the Corporation's original creation, the Corporation shall identify the complete details of its source and of any licence or other restriction (including, but not limited to, related patents, trademarks, and licence agreements) of which the Corporation is aware, and conspicuously mark the work as "Submitted on behalf of a third-party: [named here]".

## 8. Notification of Change in Circumstances

The Corporation agrees to notify Us of any facts or circumstances of which the Corporation becomes aware that would make these representations inaccurate in any respect, and to promptly update the list of Designated Employees when authority is granted or revoked.
