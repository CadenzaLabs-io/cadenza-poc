# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security vulnerabilities to **security@cadenzalabs.dev**. Do not open a public GitHub issue.

Include, where possible:

- A description of the issue and its impact.
- Repro steps or a proof-of-concept.
- Any suggested remediation.

## Disclosure timeline

We operate under **90-day coordinated disclosure**:

- We acknowledge receipt within **3 business days**.
- We provide an initial assessment within **10 business days**.
- We aim to ship a fix and coordinate public disclosure within **90 days** of the initial report. Extensions may be negotiated where a fix requires downstream coordination.

## Scope

This policy covers `cadenza-poc` (the MIT-licensed `@cadenza/sdk` reference implementation). Vulnerabilities in the production tier (`cadenza-enterprise`) or the hosted service (`cadenza-platform`) should also be reported to the same address — please note the repository in your subject line.

## Safe harbour

Good-faith research conducted under this policy will not result in legal action. Do not run destructive tests against production systems, do not access data belonging to other users, and do not exfiltrate data beyond what is necessary to demonstrate the issue.

## Recognition

We are happy to publicly credit reporters after a fix ships, unless you prefer to remain anonymous.
